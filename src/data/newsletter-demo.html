Hi there,

It is surprisingly easy to spend an evening looking for something to read—and never actually start reading.

This week, we’re keeping it simple: one book, ten quiet minutes, and no pressure to finish anything.

Open a book you’ve been curious about and give it a few pages. If it doesn’t pull you in, try another. A reading habit should have a little room for curiosity.

Here’s a place to start.

A smaller reading list

Instead of choosing by what you feel you should read, choose by what you want from the evening.

  • Something absorbing. A story that makes you forget to check the time.
  • Something brief. An essay, a poem, or a chapter you can enjoy without a long commitment.
  • Something familiar. A favourite you can open anywhere and feel at home.
Your next favourite book doesn’t have to be the one everyone else is reading.

Make it an evening, not a task

Make a cup of something. Put your phone somewhere you can’t reach it without getting up. Let the first ten minutes be enough.

You don’t need to catch up. The pages will still be there tomorrow.
